G-Scanner: The Food Scanner

G-Scanner is a free app developed by Emanuele Ciotola that helps you instantly consult food product information by scanning barcodes. Using Open Food Facts, it offers filters for:

  • Celiac Disease: Strict filter for gluten or traces.
  • Additives: Warning for modified starches and flavors.
  • Lactose Intolerance: Search for milk, butter, whey, and lactose.

Immediate result with a traffic light system (Green, Yellow, Red). Note: For informational purposes only.
